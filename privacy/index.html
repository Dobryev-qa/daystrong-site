Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

DayStrong respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is handled, and what choices you have.

1. Data We Handle

  • No account is required to use DayStrong.
  • Challenge plans, progress, streaks, and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase entitlement status is provided by Apple through StoreKit when you buy or restore Premium.
  • Notification permission status is used only to manage local workout reminders.

2. How DayStrong Uses Data

  • To save your plans, progress, reminders, and premium status.
  • To restore purchases through Apple's in-app purchase system.
  • To deliver local workout reminders when you enable them.
  • To keep the app stable and functional on your device.

3. Notifications

DayStrong can send local reminder notifications if you choose to enable them. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iPhone Settings at any time.

4. Payments and Premium Access

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. DayStrong does not store your full payment card details. Premium access and purchase records are managed through your Apple account.

5. Data Sharing

DayStrong does not sell your personal data. DayStrong does not currently require you to create an account or upload your workout history to a remote server. Apple may process purchase-related data according to Apple's own policies when you use in-app purchases.

6. Data Retention

Your workout data remains on your device unless you delete the app or remove the data from your device. Purchase records are retained by Apple according to App Store policies.

7. Children

DayStrong is not intended for children under 13.

8. Your Choices

  • You can disable notifications at any time.
  • You can manage purchases and refunds through your Apple account settings.
  • You can contact support if you have privacy questions.

9. Contact

Support email: homebody.challenge.app@gmail.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.
